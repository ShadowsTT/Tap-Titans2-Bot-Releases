Before you start

What you need

  • A computer with Windows, macOS on Apple silicon, or 64-bit Linux.
  • An Android phone with Tap Titans 2 installed and USB debugging turned on.
  • A USB data cable. Follow the setup guide to connect your phone and install the app.
