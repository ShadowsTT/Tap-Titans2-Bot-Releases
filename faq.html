Safety and privacy

What does the app send to the licence server?
While the trial or a key is in use, the app checks it with the licence server. Each check sends only: a one-way hash that identifies your computer (it cannot be turned back into anything about you), the app version, your operating system (Windows, macOS or Linux), a random number used once, and your key. The server keeps your IP address only as a salted hash. Nothing about your game account, your files or your screen is sent.
Does the app need my game account or password?
No. It only looks at the phone's screen and taps on it, the way you would.
Does this website track me?
No. It sets no cookies and has no analytics and no forms. The download page asks GitHub for the latest release list, nothing more.
Is it made by Game Hive?
No. Tap Titans 2 Bot is independent and not affiliated with Game Hive.
